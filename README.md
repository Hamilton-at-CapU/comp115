# COMP 115

Notes and labs for the COMP 115 course at Capilano University.  Most of this material is based on [*Think Python*, 3rd edition](https://greenteapress.com/wp/think-python-3rd-edition), by Allen B. Downey, which he has generously released under the [MIT](https://mit-license.org/) and [Creative Commons](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/) licences.  Modifications by Andrew Hamilton are also released under the [MIT](https://mit-license.org/) and [Creative Commons](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/) licences.
